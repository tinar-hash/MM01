Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is migraine the same thing as a bad headache?
No. Migraine is a neurological disease with distinct phases, not a more painful version of a headache. Treating it as "just a headache" is one of the most common reasons people go untreated for years.
Can migraine be cured?
There's no cure, but frequency, intensity and duration can be meaningfully reduced with the right combination of medical, lifestyle and integrative care.
Do I need a diagnosis before using Migraine Mindshift?
No. The self-assessment and Education Hub are useful whether or not you've seen a neurologist yet — though we always recommend getting a proper diagnosis.
Does Migraine Mindshift prescribe medication?
No. We never diagnose or prescribe. We work alongside your own doctor, not instead of them.
What happens on the free discovery call?
A twenty-minute conversation about what you're experiencing, with no commitment and nothing to buy on the call itself.
Is my information kept private?
